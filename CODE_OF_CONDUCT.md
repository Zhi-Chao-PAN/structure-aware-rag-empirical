# Code of Conduct for Structure-Aware RAG Research

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone involved in this research project. We value diversity and welcome contributions from individuals of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions
- Focus on constructive criticism and professional discourse
- Acknowledge and credit contributions appropriately
- Maintain confidentiality of private communications
- Be patient and helpful with newcomers

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Disrespectful or offensive comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Disruptive behavior in discussions
- Any form of professional misconduct

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer at zhichao.pan@example.com. All reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Violations of this code may result in:
- Private warnings
- Temporary suspension from project activities
- Permanent removal from the project community
